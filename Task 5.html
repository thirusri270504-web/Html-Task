Our Story

Midnight Brew started with a simple idea: create a place where people could slow down, enjoy a good cup of coffee and forget about the clock for a while.

We started small, with one coffee machine, three tables and a lot of enthusiasm. Today, we still believe that the best cafés are built around people rather than fancy things.
